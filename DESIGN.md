# Island Futures design direction

Use the original mvislandfutures.org homepage and the user-supplied reference screenshot as the visual authority. The redesign must retain the clearer career, training, funding, and application information while feeling like the same Island organization.

- Lead with the original ocean photograph, an open horizon, and a centered message about building a meaningful career close to home.
- Use deep ocean blue, white, pale coastal blue, and warm peach or sand accents. Use generous white space and relaxed Manrope/Raleway typography.
- Keep the original logo prominent, use rounded text buttons, and introduce the Career Navigator section with a broad wave.
- Preserve photographs in their full original proportions. Mobile may place text below the full ocean frame instead of cropping it.
- Never use decorative arrow icons or arrow characters in buttons, links, cards, or section calls to action. Use clear text labels. Keep functional controls such as select indicators only where needed.
- Never add decorative numbered markers to cards or sections.
- Keep career search, training filters, saved paths, funding, application guidance, and Career Navigator access easy to find.
- Guidance forms prepare an email draft; never imply that the website submitted a message.

The coastal presentation is in public/coastal.css, loaded after the foundational layout styles. Validate desktop, tablet, and mobile rendering after visual changes.
